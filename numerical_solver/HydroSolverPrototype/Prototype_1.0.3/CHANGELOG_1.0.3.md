# Prototype 1.0.3 — changes

## Schedule/output update
- `SCHEDULE` is now the single source of both well-control events and output/report times.
- Every SCHEDULE row is an exact timestep boundary and mandatory output point.
- Repeating the same BHP/RATE target only adds a report time and does not change physics.
- New control at a schedule boundary is applied starting with the next timestep.
- `t=0` is exported because it is the first schedule row.

## Regression protection
- Black-oil conservation equations: unchanged.
- Active-set phase switching: unchanged.
- PVT/relperm physics: unchanged.
- Grouped/reference finite-difference Jacobians: unchanged.
- Sparse/dense linear backend behavior: unchanged.
- Adaptive timestep growth/cut rules: unchanged.

## Validation
- 10/10 schedule/input/rate-control tests PASS.
- T00 and T01 step sequences are exactly identical to the preceding validated 1.0.3 runs.
- Common-time T00/T01 state and rate differences are at floating-point roundoff level.


## Validation bugfix: producer-only reverse-flow protection
- BHP producer now shuts in when target BHP exceeds first-cell pressure instead of injecting.
- OIL_RATE control with active minimum-BHP constraint follows the same producer-only rule.
- This fix was triggered by T08 reverse-dissolution validation.

## T05 numerical-robustness fix
- Production convergence test with N=100 and DTMAX=3 min exposed a Newton failure at day 28.0769 as a gas-active cell crossed the maximum-Rs bubble-point kink (Pb=180 bar).
- Root cause: the positive finite-difference pressure perturbation straddled the piecewise-linear Rs_sat(P) kink/clamp and contaminated the numerical Jacobian.
- Fix: gas-active pressure derivatives now use a one-sided finite-difference perturbation that remains on the same saturated-PVTO segment. Physics, residual equations, tolerances, timestep controller, and well controls are unchanged.
- The same rule is applied to grouped and reference finite-difference Jacobians.

## Local changes — GPN project (BUG-014)
- Problem: a long step after BHP is raised above the first-cell pressure did not converge at any step split. Two mechanisms: (1) the producer-only well branch (open/shut) was chosen from the current Newton iterate — the first iterate is below BHP, the well is shut, and on that branch the step has no consistent solution; (2) free gas dissolving in the near-well zone drove trial points below `Sg = -0.05`, and the line search rejected them all; the phase active set switches cells only after convergence.
- `advance()` first runs the original algorithm (now `_advance_phases()`, unchanged). If it converges, the result is bitwise the same as before.
- Only on failure, a fallback: `_sg_lower_bound = None` (no lower bound on Sg of two-phase cells — method of negative saturations; Sg < 0 is a smooth extension of the cell equations and the phase active set switches such cells after convergence). BHP control additionally fixes the well branch for the whole solve (`_well_branch`): start from the old state (`p0 <= BHP` → shut), accept only a consistent result (open → `p0 >= BHP`, shut → `p0 <= BHP`), otherwise try the other branch once. OIL_RATE control: a plain retry without the Sg bound.
- Outside `advance()` both attributes keep their defaults (`None`, `-0.05`), also after an exception (`try/finally`); `well_rates()` and `face_component_fluxes_sm3s()` choose the branch from `p0` as before.
- `BlackOilNewtonReport`: new fields `well_branch` (BHP control, branch of the accepted step), `well_switches`, `fallback` — with defaults, positional construction unchanged.
- Physics, residual equations, tolerances and well semantics (producer only, no injection) are unchanged. `PACKAGE_MANIFEST_SHA256.txt` is not updated.
- Issue: https://github.com/ArturSalamatin/Oil_Gas/issues/42; plan: `vault/plans/bug-014 well-shutin-active-set.md` of the GPN project.

## Local changes — GPN project (BUG-020): fourth stage of advance (off by default)
- Problem: shut-in (q_oil = 0, or BHP raised to the outer pressure) after production with a developed gas zone (Sg near the well 0.4, GPN examples 19-21) did not converge at any step split. (1) First step: once the well is shut, the well cell is upstream of no face, and its gas storage per unit Sg, 1/Bg - Rs/Bo, is small at low pressure (7.4 at 51 bar against 38.9 at 200 bar); the first Newton direction asks |dSg| ~ 0.7*dt[s] there. The original bound Sg >= -0.05 rejects every trial, the BUG-014 fallback overshoots to Sg = -23...-1137, the BUG-016 third stage scales the whole update by 0.2/|dSg| and the pressure stalls. (2) Later steps: in the stagnant undersaturated zone near the shut well the upstream cell of 1-11 faces flips every Newton iteration; the Newton needs ~26 iterations against the limit 15.
- `advance()`: a fourth stage, only when the three existing stages fail and only with the new constructor argument `phase_switching_stage=True` (default `False`). It is the BUG-014 fallback (`_fallback`: well branches, consistency check) with `_advance_phases` routed to the new `_newton_switching()`: the phase active set is switched after every Newton iteration (`_switch_phases()`: a two-phase cell with Sg < 0 becomes undersaturated with the Rs that keeps its gas/oil ratio; an undersaturated cell with Rs > Rs_sat becomes two-phase with the closed-flash Sg and the BUG-018 cap); updates are limited per cell (|dp| <= 0.3 p, |dSg| <= 0.2, as dpMaxRel, dsMax of OPM Flow); no line search; faces whose flow direction flipped twice keep their upstream cell (`_upwind_frozen`, read by `face_component_fluxes_sm3s`) until convergence, then a polish without holding returns the original upwind scheme; at most 40 iterations, the residual after the last update is checked; a root with two-phase Sg > 0.98 is rejected.
- With the default `phase_switching_stage=False` the prototype runs exactly the former code: regression 17/17 PASS bitwise, golden outputs unchanged. With the stage on (the GPN factory), steps the first three stages solve stay bitwise.
- `BlackOilNewtonReport`: new field `phase_switching` (the fourth stage ran), default `False`.
- Physics, residual equations, tolerances and well semantics are unchanged. `PACKAGE_MANIFEST_SHA256.txt` is not updated.

## Local changes — GPN project (DEBT-040): BHP fallback skips the 'open' branch where it cannot be consistent
- Problem: under BHP control the BUG-014 fallback (`_fallback()`, used by the second, third and fourth stages of `advance()`) tries the other well branch after the first fails. ## Local changes — GPN project (BUG-022): per-cell Newton convergence test (off by default)
- Problem: both Newton solvers (`_newton_fixed_active_set`, stages 1-3; `_newton_switching`, stage 4) stop on one absolute test for all cells, ||F||_inf <= atol + rtol*||F0||_inf with atol = 1e-11 m3st/s and the gas rows divided by `gas_residual_scale` = max(Rs_ext, 1). On the log grid the well cell has ~(r_w/r_e)^2 of the outer cell's pore volume (GPN example 17: 4.96e-3 against 1.84e4 m3), so the same residual there is a ~4e6 times larger relative error. In GPN example 17 (shut-in, q_oil = 0) the Rs of the undersaturated well cell, which can only grow (its oil comes from cell 1 only), wandered by +-0.015 a day and fell by up to 0.018 a day: the gas tolerance allowed an Rs error of up to 2.6e-3 per 1 h step.
- New constructor argument `cell_tol` (default `None`: the test is off). With it a Newton solve converges only when, besides the absolute test, every cell meets max_c |F_c,i|*dt/PV_i <= cell_tol (F = `residual()`, PV_i = pore volume of `state_old`); otherwise the solve fails and `advance` tries its next stage. For an undersaturated cell upstream of no face (the shut-in well cell) its Rs enters the storage only, and the Rs error of a step is estimated by cell_tol*Bo*(gas_residual_scale + Rs). New methods `_cell_weights`, `_cells_converged`. Far below ~eps_mach*PV_max/PV_min the test is not met: at cell_tol = 1e-10, GPN example 17 rejects 214 steps against 47 and needs 2.9 times the Jacobians (both the line search and the stage-4 Newton stall; the mechanism is not established). The estimate is wrong in form: see DEBT-055 below.
- With the default `cell_tol=None` the prototype runs exactly the former code: tests 97 passed, regression 17/17 PASS bitwise, golden outputs unchanged. The GPN factory passes `numerics.newton_cell_tol` (default 1e-8): example 17's Rs at the well no longer decreases by more than 2e-5 between records; 21 of 22 GPN examples change (18 at round-off), Jacobians +13 %.
- Physics, residual equations, the absolute test and its tolerances, line searches and well semantics are unchanged. `PACKAGE_MANIFEST_SHA256.txt` is not updated.

## Local changes — GPN project (BUG-025): absolute Newton tolerance not below the round-off floor
- Problem: the residual is in m3st/s, its storage term is the difference of two inventories divided by dt, and each inventory carries a round-off error ~eps*M. Below ~eps*M_max/dt the residual does not go down, and at short steps this is above atol = 1e-11 m3st/s (in GPN below ~0.01-0.05 s at N = 350, ~0.5 s at N = 50). In a GPN shut-in after 10 days of production with a developed gas zone (N = 350, r_e = 200 m, M_max = 7.2e3 m3st) the line search of stages 1-3 stalled at |F|*dt = 5-7e-13 m3st and the fourth stage ran 40 iterations at the floor for dt = 1e-2...1e-4 s; the first shut-in step of 0.0549 s failed at every split down to dt/512. At N = 50 (GPN example 15) a production substep of 0.44 s failed the same way.
- New method `_absolute_tol` and module constant `ROUNDOFF_KAPPA = 4.0`: both Newton solvers (`_newton_fixed_active_set`, `_newton_switching`) test ||F||_inf <= max(atol, ROUNDOFF_KAPPA*eps*M_max/dt) + rtol*max(||F0||_inf, 1e-9), M_max -- the largest cell inventory of `state_old` (oil rows; gas rows divided by `gas_residual_scale`, as in `residual`), from the inventory cache. The measured stall is (0.07-0.7)*eps*M_max, so a solve accepted at the floor errs by at most 4*eps*M_max m3st per step. `residual_tol` of the report is this tolerance.
- Where the floor is below atol, `max` returns atol itself: prototype tests 101 passed, regression 11/11 PASS quick and 17/17 PASS full, bitwise (smallest atol*dt/(eps*M_max) over the regression cases 34, T05S_N200_DT5m). In GPN all 23 examples are bitwise; the shut-in step converges with the first stage down to dt = 1e-4 s at r_e = 50, 200, 800 m.
- Physics, residual equations, the per-cell test, line searches and well semantics are unchanged. The distribution version stays `1.0.3+gpn.1`. `PACKAGE_MANIFEST_SHA256.txt` is not updated.

## Local changes — GPN project (DEBT-055): rounding limit of the per-cell measure in a failure report
- Problem: the BUG-022 entry above estimates the limit below which the per-cell test cannot be met as ~eps_mach*PV_max/PV_min ~ eps_mach*(r_e/r_w)^2. The estimate is wrong in form. The unknowns are representable to ulp(z) only, and the residual of cell i cannot be made smaller than a share of (|J| @ ulp(z))_i. GPN probes: from 17 converged steps a pure Newton reached 0.09-0.26 of the per-cell measure of |J| @ ulp(z), failed solves stopped at 0.23-0.40 of it; a random +-1 ulp shift of z changes the measure by the same amount within 12 %. The pressure columns give 97-100 % of it, the face-flux term ~eps_mach*p*T*lambda*dt/PV_i: the limit grows as dt (x4.0 from 900 to 3600 s; x0.50000 from 86400 to 43200 s) and, near the well of the log grid, as N^2/ln^2(r_e/r_w) (GPN example 17, shut-in step of 3600 s: N = 100, 200, 400 -- 1.4e-10, 6.0e-10, 2.5e-9; r_e = 200, 1000, 2000 m -- 6.0e-10, 4.0e-10, 3.4e-10, while PV_max/PV_min grows 100 times). Step splitting lowers it; at cell_tol = 1e-8 only N = 350 with daily steps meets it (GPN BUG-018 case, example 14, a daily shut-in: 5 failures). A failure report did not show it.
- `BlackOilNewtonReport`: new field `cell_floor` -- the per-cell measure of |J| @ ulp(z) for the iterate reported, J the last Jacobian of the solve (for the 'max_iter' exit of `_newton_fixed_active_set`, the Jacobian of the iterate before the last update); only on a failed solve with `cell_tol` set, otherwise None. New method `_cell_floor`; `_newton_report` takes the Jacobian and the iterate; `advance()` carries the field of the original algorithm (DEBT-030). One sparse matrix-vector product per failed solve, no additional Jacobians.

In GPN five runs (BUG-018 at N = 350, examples 14, 16, 17, a daily shut-in) reproduce their histories and step sequences bitwise; the failure message names the limit and, where a per-cell failure is at it, says so (5 of 11 failures). The limit of the per-cell test grows with dt, the round-off floor of the absolute test (BUG-025 above) -- as 1/dt.

## Local changes — GPN project (BUG-028): passes that only switch gas off are not counted
